Aux / HVAC Forecast Model

Forecast the plant's parasitic draw (HVAC + controls) at 5-min steps up to 24 h ahead (P10 / P50 / P90): a grey-box heat-load / COP model per site plus LightGBM quantile models on its residual. View forecasts on Aux / HVAC Energy Forecast.
