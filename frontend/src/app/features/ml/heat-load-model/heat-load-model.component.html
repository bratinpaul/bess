Heat Load Model

Heat the HVAC must reject: the enclosure heat balance (battery ohmic + entropic, PCS, aux electronics, solar, envelope, thermal mass) calibrated to a measured reference, an operational forecast by issue vintage (dispatch plan + weather → RC grey-box + LightGBM quantile residual) and the thermal-design sizing. Results go to the Heat Load & Thermal Design viewer.
