Inputs

Physics: SOC % per kWh of setpoint, fitted separately for charge and discharge per EMS (their ratio is the effective round trip), plus idle drift and the SOC limits.
Plans: the dispatch schedule (dispatch_event, typical day where no command) and the typical-day setpoint profile.
Residual model: SOC now and its rate of change, setpoint, mode, grid service, AGC, aux & HVAC load, target SOC, physics SOC, planned charge / discharge energy, time of day, EMS.
